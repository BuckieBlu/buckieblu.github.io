The UX/UI

Lost life animations

As part of the UX and HUD design, I created the interface for player health feedback, which triggers when an enemy catches the player.

I prototyped the first UI concepts and animations in Canva for fast iteration on layout and motion, then rebuilt them in Unreal Engine 5 with UMG and Blueprints to animate the skulls and sync the transitions with gameplay.

The HUD

Score, lives and streaks, always in view.

The HUD gamifies the experience, showing the player their score and lives as well as their position on the map.

Lives, score and runes

  • Lives the player has left (3 in the example)
  • Current score (0 in the example)
  • Runes still to collect (330/330 in the example)

Streak multipliers

  • Rune streak: multiplies the score you get from collecting runes (x1.0 in the example)
  • Kill streak: multiplies the score you get from killing Baelorns during the power-up (x1.0 in the example)
Designing the enemy

Enemies you hear before you see.

I contributed to the enemy gameplay design, focusing on behaviour patterns, player interaction and spatial awareness cues. The diagram shows our first plan for how the enemy would perceive and react to the player in the labyrinth.

Because the maze limits what the player can see to narrow corridors, we gave the enemies distinct, heavy footsteps. Players locate threats by sound instead of sight, which builds tension and fits the horror atmosphere.

Early iterations explored a transformation where enemies switched from walking upright to sprinting on all fours once they spotted the player. It was cut for scope, since it needed extra animation.

Runes & power-ups

Runes

I designed the rune collection system, the main objective and scoring mechanic. Inspired by the pellets in Pac-Man, runes are spread across the labyrinth to encourage exploration and risk versus reward.

Each rune adds to your score, and consecutive pickups within one life build a rune streak multiplier. Players are pushed to collect more per life, while the risk of losing their streak on death keeps the pressure on.

Power-ups

The power-up turns night into day and hands the player a double-barrelled shotgun. In daylight the Baelorns turn into cute, silly little creatures that you can shoot for a big score boost.
